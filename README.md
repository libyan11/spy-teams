# Spy Teams

A team word party game for phones, part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

Two teams, Red and Blue, and a grid of 25 words. Each team's spymaster can see which words belong to which team, which are nobody's, and which one is the trap. Spymasters take turns giving a one-word clue and a number, and their teammates try to pick the right words. The first team to find all their words wins. Pick the trap and you lose on the spot.

- Teammates point at words, and everyone sees the pointers live. Tap again to pick.
- Clues can't be a word on the board, and the game checks that for you.
- English or Arabic word boards, about 300 words each, with no repeats until you've seen them all.
- Optional turn timer of 60, 90 or 120 seconds.
- If a spymaster drops out, a teammate can take over. Late arrivals join the smaller team.
- 4 to 16 players, each on their own phone.

Play: https://libyan11.github.io/spy-teams/

Built from `spy-teams.html` with `build-spy-teams.sh`.
